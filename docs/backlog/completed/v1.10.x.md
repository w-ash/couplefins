# v1.10.x: Year-Scoped Settle Up

For strategic overview, see the [planning overview](README.md).

## Why this version

The running ledger (v1.7.5) made settlement balance-forward: one all-time
outstanding number, payments applied FIFO to the oldest open month. Correct
accounting, but the Settle Up page grew unreadable as history accumulated —
one hero number spanning every month since launch, a Months list that never
stopped growing, and a payment history mixing years.

This version reframes the whole page around **one calendar year at a time**.
The ledger math is untouched: year slices are derived from the same per-month
FIFO remainders and sum back to the all-time outstanding balance. Only the
lens changes.

---

### v1.10.0: Year-Scoped Settle Up
**Goal**: Every number on Settle Up belongs to one selected calendar year — no all-time figures anywhere on the page.
**Status**: Completed (2026-08-23)
**Context**: Year slices partition the ledger exactly, because a month's `remaining` is what FIFO left unpaid. Summing a year's remainders direction-aware gives that year's balance; summing all years gives the all-time balance. That invariant is what lets the page be year-scoped without a second source of truth.
**What this unlocks**: A page that stays legible after five years of history, and a waive action that forgives one year rather than everything.

- [x] **Waive one year, not the whole ledger**
    - Effort: M
    - What: `RecordWaivedSettlementCommand` gains `waive_year`; when set, the use case forgives that year's `year_remaining_result` and scopes the missing-upload warning to `year_open_span`. Omitting it keeps the all-time behaviour. Plumbed through `RecordWaivedSettlementRequest` → `POST /settlements/waive`.
    - Why: A year-scoped page must not offer a button that silently wipes other years.
    - Dependencies: Year slices
    - Status: Completed (2026-08-23)
    - Files: `src/application/use_cases/record_waived_settlement.py`, `src/interface/api/schemas/settlements.py`, `src/interface/api/routes/settlements.py`, `tests/unit/application/test_record_waived_settlement.py`
    - Notes: Guarded — the waive is refused while an **older** year still carries a balance ("Settle or waive 2025 first"). A waiver is a payment against the ledger and FIFO relieves the oldest open month first, so waiving 2026 over an open 2025 would clear the wrong year. Waiving the oldest open year (the normal case) lands exactly where picked. The chat tool's `waive_settlement` still sends no `waive_year` and forgives the whole ledger — backward-compatible, but the two surfaces now mean different things by "waive the balance".
